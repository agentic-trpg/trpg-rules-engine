# Capability matrix — what Nat20 resolves

Nat20 is a working engine with real gaps. This page is the honest inventory:
what it resolves mechanically, what it accepts but treats as a narrative no-op,
and what it does not model at all. **If a rule is not listed as resolved here,
assume it is not enforced.**

Anything marked *not modelled* is tracked in
[`BACKLOG.md`](https://github.com/tapestria/nat20/blob/main/BACKLOG.md), which is
the authoritative gap ledger. The counts below are measured against the shipped
`dnd5e-srd-data` corpus and pinned by
`packages/dnd5e-engine/tests/test_capability_matrix.py`, so they cannot go stale
without failing CI.

!!! tip "The fastest way to check one thing"

    Content resolves if it carries an activity of a *mechanical* kind — `attack`,
    `damage`, `save`, `heal`, `check`, `cast`, or a `utility` activity carrying
    effect riders or a reviewed persistent environmental source. Execution also
    requires Admission of the selected activity; a mechanical kind alone is
    insufficient. Bare utility and unsupported alternatives remain fail closed.
    The execution audit below records each admitted operation and its limitations.

| Attack rolls, crits, damage, resistances/immunities/vulnerabilities | ⚠️ Partial | Advantage/disadvantage is rolled on **activity** attacks: attacker `flags.advantage.attack` / `flags.disadvantage.attack` effects plus the condition-derived sources (Invisible attacker; Blinded/Poisoned/Frightened/Restrained attacker; Paralyzed/Stunned/Unconscious/Blinded target), cancelling per SRD §Advantage and Disadvantage. Opportunity attacks resolve through the same activity context as any other attack (C24), so every source here reaches them. Prone (advantage within 5 ft, disadvantage beyond) and Grappled (disadvantage vs anyone but the grappler) are live (C12); the distance-derived sources are now all live: unseen attacker (C16b), Ranged Attacks in Close Combat (C15) and long-range disadvantage (C15) each append their own `AdvantageSource`. Attack proficiency is a real gate (C15): a host that never sets `PartyMemberSpec.weapon_proficiencies` is assumed proficient (legacy behavior, unchanged); an explicit (possibly empty) list enforces proficiency by the weapon's category or slug, omitting Proficiency Bonus (never subtracting it) when unproficient. `DamageApplied` now carries `source_id` (weapon slug / synthesized activity id / `mastery:<slug>`) and `is_crit` for weapon-attack damage (C15); shared spell delivery now also supplies deterministic child spell/activity `source_id` and separate parent invocation identity, while external, environmental and manual callers can still omit activity identity; a critical hit against a target already making death saves counts as two failures (SRD §Damage at 0 Hit Points, C15). Magic weapons and spells overcome resistance to *nonmagical* B/P/S (`Weapon.magical`; host-authored resistances default to the nonmagical reading via `physical_resistances_nonmagical_only`). |
| Saving throws, half-on-save, save-for-effect | ✅ Resolved | Activity saves, typed repeats, Grapple/Shove, concentration and Undead Fortitude share resolved ability/proficiency modifiers, condition auto-fail/advantage/disadvantage, Exhaustion, effect bonus dice and armed Legendary Resistance. Monster scores/proficiencies hydrate from templates. Generic next-save disadvantage consumes once even when canceled, successful or automatically failed; death saves share that consumer. Magic Resistance requires spell or explicit magical provenance. Standalone legacy saving-throw checks lack live one-use consumption. [Save-surface audit](dev/effect-lifecycle.md). |
| Ability & skill checks (in and out of combat) | ✅ Resolved (bounded scope) | Typed CheckRequest and a generic check Action feed one deterministic resolver shared by Hide, Escape and canonical CheckActivity. Real live ability/PB/proficiency/Expertise, Jack of All Trades, Reliable Talent, noisy armor, effects and declarative condition penalties apply. Explicit sense requirements support zero-draw auto-fail; social semantics and condition lineage gate charmer Advantage. Help and sunlight compose through AdvantageSources. Search/Study/Influence are audited Action wrappers; DM/Agent still chooses check, skill, DC and semantic requirements. Engine performs no narrative/DC judgment. Standalone resolve_check APIs remain available. See [typed boundary and RNG discipline](dev/typed-ability-checks.md). |
| Action economy (action, bonus action, reaction, movement) | ⚠️ Partial | Extra Attack (`attacks_remaining` per Action, `LiveCombatView.turn`) and Light/Nick off-hand attacks are modelled (C14/C15). Character Action Economy shares a turn-continuation helper for remaining Attack swings, available Action Surge uses/slots, paid Flurry strikes, Martial Arts and Cunning Action Bonus Actions, and Light/Nick windows; available movement after Bonus Actions/free activations and Extra Attack remains usable. `pass` always ends the turn. Fighter 2–4 can Attack then Surge, and Monk 1–4 can Attack then use the Bonus Unarmed Strike or, from Monk 2, Flurry. Action Surge grants one restricted non-Magic Action (`LiveCombatView.turn.extra_actions_remaining`), once per turn: eligible actions spend that slot first, preserving the unrestricted base Action for Magic in either order. Refused casts preserve action budgets, slots, pending reactions, RNG and the current turn. Countered casts spend their casting-time Action/Bonus Action/Reaction but preserve the spell slot and any unused Action Surge extra Action. **Still partial:** the corpus lacks reliable feature/item Magic-action classification, so Action-costed `use_feature`/`use_item` remain conservatively excluded from the extra slot. Flurry spends one Focus Point and the Bonus Action once for two Unarmed Strikes (three with Heightened Focus); paid strikes survive intervening Actions. Martial Arts funds damage, Grapple or Shove as a Bonus Unarmed Strike (`use_bonus_action=True`), and pending Flurry strikes fund all three. Cunning Action funds Bonus-Action Dash, Disengage and Hide. Loading permits one shot per Attack action, including a fresh action from Surge; Bonus Action/Reaction payments are separate. Empty/unknown weapon attacks are refused before any budget or attack-sequence spend. Incapacitated blocks action/bonus/reaction intents (C12). |
| Dash, Disengage | ✅ Resolved | The Action by default; the Bonus Action through Cunning Action (`use_bonus_action=True`), read from the granted features at each class's own level — a Rogue 2 or any build with two Rogue levels, not `class_slug == "rogue"` (C20) |

| Help | ✅ Resolved | Attack Help keeps its existing one-use Advantage and helper-next-turn expiry. The next matching ability check consumes one grant even if Advantage cancels or it auto-fails; unrelated checks preserve it. Tool inventory/complete tool Help remains deferred. |
| Hide | ✅ Resolved | Gated on Three-Quarters/Total cover, Heavy obscurement, or Darkness on the hider's own cell (SRD 5.2 glossary: "An area of darkness is Heavily Obscured", `GridTopology.light_on_cell`); the hider must also be out of every living, non-Incapacitated hostile's line of sight (C16b `_combatant_can_see`) — skipped only when the hider's own cell already carries Three-Quarters/Total cover, since per-cell cover is omnidirectional and already breaks every line (SRD 5.2 §Cover). Costs an Action by default; `use_bonus_action=True` requires granted Cunning Action and spends the Bonus Action. Illegal payment or visibility gates reject before any roll or state change; a failed Stealth check still pays. Each further attempt needs another payment. Shared typed check resolution includes noisy armor Disadvantage, Jack of All Trades, Reliable Talent and declarative check penalties. |
| Opportunity attacks | ✅ Resolved | Every walk, in both directions (C24): a `move` intent (a character's or a host-driven foe's), the monster AI's closing walk and its flee. A step provokes when it takes the mover from inside a reactor's reach to outside it, so stepping around inside reach never does; Disengage suppresses it from either side, and a summon makes none (BACKLOG.md). The walk stops where an attack drops the mover to 0 HP or its Speed to 0. The attack resolves through the same activity context as one on the reactor's own turn: a character swings `PartyMemberSpec.opportunity_attack_weapon_id`, else the first melee weapon in its `equipment`, else an Unarmed Strike; a monster or a transformed creature makes the first non-ranged attack on its stat block. Cover, Fighting Styles, Martial Arts, proficiency, enchantments, weapon mastery, Sneak Attack, the C16b "can see" trigger and carve-outs, and the target's readied Shield all apply; Cleave, the Light property's extra attack, Loading and Bardic Inspiration do not, and a Versatile weapon always swings its one-handed die — the opportunity-attack path never sets `use_versatile_damage`, so Great Weapon Fighting doesn't reach it either (BACKLOG.md). Reach is the weapon's (10 ft with the Reach property), a stat-block melee attack's own range, else `reach_ft` (5 ft by default). A mover a Push mastery hit carries off stops where the push leaves it, keeping its unspent movement, rather than finishing the step the push interrupted |

## Spells

Combat spell execution now requires a shared, pure admission preflight before
payment, concentration replacement or a Counterspell opportunity. PC, monster
and delegated item casts use the same reviewed capability records. A resolver
event or an Activity kind is not evidence that an entire spell is supported.

| Admission classification | Spells | Contract |
|---|---|---|
| Executable | **30** | Reviewed combat creature payload within the engine's existing spatial/host boundary. |
| Bounded | **48** | Explicit selected Activity contract; limitations and independent deferred alternatives remain recorded. |
| Host narrative | **26** | Host adjudicates the narrative/world result; `SpellCast.execution_class` explicitly identifies this boundary. |
| Deferred | **235** | Required mechanics are missing; refuse before Action, slot, charge, concentration or RNG. |

The [generated inventory](audits/spell-execution.json) covers all **339 spells /
464 Activities**, including default selection, alternative/delayed/persistent
roles, missing mechanisms and entrypoint restrictions. It is static evidence,

## Reactions

Reactions work, with one constraint that shapes any host integration:

> **The engine never pauses mid-resolution to ask whether you want to react.**
> A creature must *pre-arm* its reaction on its own turn with a `"ready"`
> intent. The engine then fires it automatically when the trigger occurs.

| Reaction | Status |
|---|---|
| Opportunity attack | ✅ Resolved — needs no arming: every walk that leaves an enemy's reach draws it, whoever drives the mover (a `move` intent, the monster AI's closing walk or its flee); Disengage suppresses it from either side, and a summon makes none (BACKLOG.md) |
| Shield (incl. vs. Magic Missile) | ✅ Resolved — a provisional hit opens the shared attack hook; the original roll produces one final AttackRolled. Typed targeted-spell metadata negates the triggering Magic Missile's damage only. |
| Counterspell | ✅ Resolved — slot-gated at the readied level and 60 ft range/LoS-gated at drain time (an ineligible reactor's armed reaction is skipped, not popped) |
| Hellish Rebuke | ✅ Resolved — a positive complete damage instance opens the typed damage trigger; the real living source must be visible and in range, and the reactor must still be able to react. Multi-type parts produce one opportunity. |
| Readied spell cast | ✅ Resolved — a supported canonical reaction spell derives its conditions automatically and needs an unexpended slot at its readied level; an ineligible reactor's armed reaction is skipped, not popped. Unsupported or incompatible declarations are refused before Action payment. |
| Feather Fall falling trigger | ❌ Not modelled — canonical CREATURE_FALLS exists, but the engine has no authoritative falling opportunity producer, so pre-arm is refused. |
| Arbitrary SRD "Ready an action with a custom trigger" | ❌ Not modelled |

The optional `reaction_trigger` input is a deprecated compatibility check; it
cannot add a trigger absent from the canonical conditions. Matching conditions
use OR semantics and eligible reactors fire in initiative order. Rage and
transformation keep blocked declarations armed. Nested reaction chains and a
monster AI reaction policy remain deferred. Absorb Elements is absent from the
bundled canonical spell corpus; no runtime spell-name branch is inferred for it.
The [reaction design](dev/reaction-queue.md) links the complete deterministic
inventory and its generation command.

## Environment & exploration

Deliberately out of scope for a combat engine, and not modelled: falling damage,
suffocation, mounted combat, underwater combat, improvised weapons, object
interaction, and encumbrance.

## Event stream

Every call returns typed `CombatEvent`s.

`AttackRolled`, `SaveRolled`, `CheckRolled` and `ConcentrationCheck` all
carry the roll breakdown: `natural` (the die kept after advantage/disadvantage),
`modifier` (the flat bonus) and `sources` (which advantage/disadvantage sources
applied) — so a host can render "14 + 5 = 19". Two residual limits:

- The target's effective AC is not reported, so a miss cannot be explained as
  "vs AC 16"; and `modifier` excludes Bless/Bane-style bonus DICE, which must be
  rolled after the d20 to keep the seeded stream stable.
- `DamageApplied` names what dealt it (`source_id`: a weapon slug, a
  synthesized activity id or `mastery:<slug>`) only for weapon-attack damage;
  spell, save and heal damage still report `None` in that field. Shared activity
  damage separately carries `source_actor_id` and `damage_instance_id`, so hosts
  can identify its actor and group the parts of one damage instance.

`TurnPhase(phase, actor_id, round_number)` marks each turn boundary —
`round_start` / `turn_start` / `turn_end` — immediately before the engine runs
that phase's lifecycle hooks, so "top of round 3" and "end of Alice's turn" are
readable directly rather than inferred from `TurnStarted`/`TurnEnded` adjacency.

`ConcentrationCheck(target_id, dc, roll_total, succeeded, …)` is the only event
for the damage-triggered concentration save: no `SaveRolled` accompanies it, so a
host that counts saves counts both types — see
[the v0.6 → v0.7 migration guide](migration/v0.6-to-v0.7.md).

Every residual limit in that list is tracked in `BACKLOG.md`.
